    Why does my Mac ask?

    Apple charges developers $99 a year to register an app. This is a free project, so macOS asks you to confirm it the first time. After that it opens like any other app.

    Questions

    Is it really free?

    Yes. No trial, no subscription, no ads. It was made for friends and is shared as it is.

    Does it need the internet?

    Only to sign in the first time. After that everything works offline, and changes sync when you're back online.

    What happens to my data?

    Your sessions and settings go to your own account, with a copy on each device you're signed in on. Otherwise the app only sends its version number, so updates reach everyone. Nothing is sold or shared.

    Which Macs?

    Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and newer). Intel Macs aren't supported yet.

    How do updates work?

    A notice appears in the app. Click Update now; it reopens on the new version and shows what changed.

    Windows? Android? iPhone?

    All three. There's a Windows version, an Android app, and an iPhone app you add from Safari (it works offline too). Sign in on each with the same account and your history follows you.
